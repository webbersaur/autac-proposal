PRODUCT AND SERVICE DESCRIPTION (1,338 characters, limit 2,000)
Autac, Inc. has manufactured retractile (coiled) cords in Connecticut since 1947 and is 100% woman-owned and WBENC certified. We stock the world's largest inventory of retractile power cords, with over 400 UL and CSA listed part numbers ready to ship, most within one business day, with no minimum order.

Products: retractile power cords, coiled cords, curly cords, shielded and EMI-protected coil cords, miniature coiled cords, test lead cords, handset and communication cords, straight and retractile cord sets with molded NEMA plugs and IEC connectors, and custom cable assemblies. Jackets in TPE, TPR (thermoplastic rubber), PVC, polyurethane and Auta-Prene. 2 to 10 conductors, 26 to 10 AWG, retracted lengths from 12 to 48 inches, custom colors available.

Services: custom coiled cords built to your specifications with no minimum quantity, coil-only service for customer-supplied cable, and in-house straight wire for fast turnaround on custom orders.

Industries served: aerospace and defense, medical and dental equipment, industrial machinery and controls, automotive and vehicle, marine, telecommunications, test and measurement, emergency lighting, and OEMs nationwide.

Certifications: UL Listed, CSA Certified, RoHS and REACH compliant, CAGE code 3AWW0, SAM registered. All products are made in Branford, Connecticut, USA.
